About Kepler
College

At Kepler College, students learn by applying knowledge, demonstrating their skills through real-world projects and case studies rooted in Rwandan and regional contexts. Each course is practical, designed to equip students with the in-demand skills employers want, preparing them to succeed both locally and globally. Regardless of learners’ primary subject, Kepler College students are equipped with 21st-century, transferable skills that help them work across sectors.
