About Kepler
College

At Kepler College, students learn by applying knowledge, demonstrating their skills through real-world projects and case studies rooted in Rwandan and regional contexts. Each course is practical, designed to equip students with the in-demand skills employers want, preparing them to succeed both locally and globally. Regardless of learners’ primary subject, Kepler College students are equipped with 21st-century, transferable skills that help them work across sectors.

History

Kepler established Kepler College to scale its impactful education programs that have transformed the lives of thousands and their families. We received accrediation from the government of Rwanda in 2022.

Academic Programs

At Kepler College, students learn by actively engaging in real-world projects and case studies based on Rwandan and regional contexts. Each course is practical, aimed at providing students with the high-demand skills that employers want. Every program is hands-on, preparing students to succeed both locally and worldwide.
